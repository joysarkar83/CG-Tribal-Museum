Chhattisgarh Tribal Museum

A place of Tribal Culture, Tradition and Heritage.

About the Museum

Step into a world where ancient traditions come alive. Our Tribal Heritage Museum celebrates the rich cultural Heritage of indigenous communities, preserving their wisdom, art, and stories for future generations. Through carefully collected exhibitions, we invite you to explore the profound connection between tribal peoples and their natural environment, their spiritual beliefs, and their unique artistic expressions.

Our Collection

Our collection features over 10,000 carefully preserved artifacts, each telling a unique story of indigenous heritage. From intricately crafted ceremonial masks and traditional weapons to healing herbs and musical instruments, every item represents the ingenuity, spirituality, and artistic expression of tribal communities. Special exhibits highlight the sustainable practices that have allowed these cultures to thrive in harmony with nature for thousands of years.

Visiting Experience

Immerse yourself in the vibrant traditions of tribal peoples through our interactive exhibits, cultural demonstrations, and educational programs. Witness traditional crafting techniques, participate in storytelling sessions, and experience the rhythmic power of tribal music. Our knowledgeable guides, many from tribal communities themselves, offer insights that bring each artifact and story to life.
