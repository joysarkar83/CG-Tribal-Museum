Messages from Our Leaders

Message from the Hon'ble President

It is a matter of joy that 'Shaheed Veer Narayan Singh Memorial cum Tribal Freedom Fighters Museum' is being inaugurated by the Department of Tribal Welfare, Government of Chhattisgarh in collaboration with the Government of India on November 01, 2025. The book “Adi Shaurya” is also being published on this occasion.

The establishment of 'Shaheed Veer Narayan Singh Memorial cum Tribal Freedom Fighters Museum' is commendable. This initiative showcases the patriotism, valor and sacrifice of the tribal communities. Along with historical importance, this museum will also be helpful in awakening social consciousness. I am confident that the book 'Adi Shaurya' will act as an important document for the tribal communities.

I appreciate all the people associated with this program and the book and wish it a successful publication. The establishment of this museum represents a commitment to safeguarding the unique identity and cultural legacy of tribal communities. Through these galleries, we can witness the deep connection between tribal societies and nature, their sustainable practices, and their invaluable contributions to our nation's diversity.
Smt. Droupadi Murmu

Message from the Hon'ble Prime Minister

India's strength lies in its diversity, and our tribal communities are custodians of ancient knowledge, traditions, and a deep understanding of living in harmony with nature. This museum stands as a testament to the rich cultural heritage of Chhattisgarh's tribal population and their significant contributions to our nation.

The tribal communities have maintained their unique identity while contributing to the nation's progress. Their art, music, traditions, and sustainable lifestyle offer valuable lessons for modern society. It is essential that we preserve and promote this heritage for future generations to understand and appreciate.

I congratulate the state government and all those involved in establishing this museum. May it serve as an inspiration for similar initiatives across the country and help in creating awareness about the invaluable heritage of our tribal communities.
Shri Narendra Modi

Message from the Hon'ble Governor

Tribal resistance has played an important and long-term influential role in India's freedom struggle. Many years before the great revolution of 1857, the British government had reached out to the inaccessible tribal settlements and exploited the priceless heritage of the area, due to which the tribal area had also become disturbed.

If we look at the history from the Middle Ages to the present, a large and unbroken chain of revolutions of the tribes is visible in a peace-loving region like Chhattisgarh. To protect their freedom and identity, the tribes carried out revolution with traditional weapons. Even if any resistance stopped or was suppressed, it did not mean that the seeds of dissatisfaction against the British policies could not germinate again in the minds of the tribes.

Historian and writer Lord Acton's statement 'Constant awareness is the price of freedom' is a reflection of the constant awareness of the tribal communities living in forested or inaccessible areas towards the protection of their culture, lifestyle, life values ​​and natural habitat. Today I am happy that, to make the general public aware of the bravery and valor of the tribal leaders of Chhattisgarh in the form of tribal pride, the book 'Adi Shaurya' has been published under the documentation of the theme of establishing “Shaheed Veer Narayan Singh Memorial cum Tribal Freedom Fighters Museum”. Chhattisgarh Government, Tribal Development Department and Tribal Research and Training Institute deserve congratulations for this effort.
Shri Ramen Deka

Message from the Hon'ble Union Cabinet Minister of Tribal Affairs

I am extremely happy to know that a coffee table book “Adi Shaurya” dedicated to the grandeur of Shaheed Veer Narayan Singh Memorial cum Tribal Freedom Fighters Museum and the unforgettable sacrifice of tribal heroes is going to be published.

This book is not only a collectible work but also an important attempt to highlight the bravery of the tribal communities and their decisive contribution to India's freedom struggle. The lives and valor of great heroes like Shaheed Veer Narayan Singh remind us how our tribal heroes sacrificed their lives for the protection of water, forests, land and the freedom of the nation.

This coffee table book will connect our young generation with these precious stories through visual medium, and inspire them to be proud of their roots and glorious history. I convey my heartiest congratulations for the success of 'Adi Shaurya' and the wide dissemination of stories of tribal freedom fighters through it.
Shri Jual Oram

Message from the Hon'ble Cheif Minister

Chhattisgarh is a state enriched with tribal culture and heritage. It is home to 43 tribal groups, each with its distinct culture, housing patterns, attire, adornments, religious beliefs, customs, dance and music, and forest-based livelihood practices.

Along with the personal and communal development of the tribal communities in the state, preserving their traditional cultural heritage for future generations is one of our top priorities.

I am extremely pleased that under the guidance of the Chhattisgarh Government and the Tribal Development Department, the Tribal Research and Training Institute has established the state's first Tribal Museum dedicated to the tribal communities and their cultural legacy. I hope the tribal cultures and lifestyles showcased in this museum, along with the information made available through digital platforms, will offer an opportunity for not only the state's tribal communities, students, and scholars, but also people from other states to understand and appreciate Chhattisgarh’s tribal heritage.
Shri Vishnu Deo Sai

Message from the Principal Secretary

It is indeed a matter of great pleasure and pride that the Chhattisgarh government, through the Tribal and Scheduled Caste Development Department, has established the state's first tribal museum dedicated to preserving and promoting the tribal communities' rich cultural heritage. This includes their cultural distinctiveness, traditions, beliefs, religious faiths, values, language–dialects, economic life, traditional knowledge and practices, and more.

The tribal population constitutes a significant portion of Chhattisgarh, and 43 scheduled tribes of the state, along with their sub-tribes, hold an important place in Indian social and cultural diversity. In today's rapidly evolving lifestyle with modern communication means, various traditions and cultural aspects are being influenced. Preserving the cultural identity, customs, and traditions of tribal communities poses new challenges. In this context, the importance of tribal museums increases even more.

Various aspects of tribal life in the state are depicted in different dimensions - such as their lifestyle, culture, tribal dwellings, households, traditional tools, food-gathering practices like hunting–fishing–agriculture, clothing, ornaments, and personal hygiene practices. Exhibits include folk art, music, religious beliefs, and faith in supernatural and natural powers. There is also information on gold and red soil-based special identities like Godna (tattooing), traditional technology, and socio-cultural festivals, along with displays on tribal fairs and weekly markets as major centers of socialization. Special attention has been given to the state's particularly vulnerable tribal groups, and their lifestyle is showcased through digital media in 14 galleries, corridors, and courtyards.

In digital form, the museum also features audiovisual recordings, fieldwork, subject-specific 3D displays, interactive models of tribal habitats and homes, street theatre, kiosks, smart displays, holograms, and holographs. Visitors can also capture their experiences through photography. Indeed, the establishment of this tribal museum will prove to be a milestone in preserving and promoting the tribal communities' culture, lifestyle, and folklore. This initiative will not only be a reference center for researchers and students within the state and country but will also help spread awareness and knowledge about tribal societies. This museum will serve as a major hub for such awareness and knowledge sharing with the general public.
Shri Sonmoni Borah

Message from the Commissioner

It is a moment of immense pride for us that the Chhattisgarh government, through the Tribal and Scheduled Caste Development Department, has established the state's first tribal museum with the objective of preserving, promoting, and developing the invaluable socio-cultural heritage of the state's tribal communities. The museum showcases various aspects of tribal lifestyles, their customs, traditions, and culture to provide knowledge to the general public through subject-wise gallery-based visual displays.

For the state of Chhattisgarh, the Government of India has recognized 43 tribal communities and their subgroups as scheduled tribes. In this tribal museum, the diverse cultures, arts and crafts, beliefs, and traditions of the state's tribal groups, and especially the Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs), are presented.

I am fully confident that this first tribal museum of the state, through its subject-based presentation, will prove useful in preserving and promoting the tribal culture of the state, and will also benefit future generations, students, researchers, and the general public in gaining knowledge about tribal communities.
Dr. Saransh Mittar

Message from the Director

The proud land of Chhattisgarh has borne witness to tribal heroes who displayed immense courage and self-sacrifice to protect their jal, jungle, and zameen as well as their inherent dignity. The spark of their struggle kindled the flame of freedom that guided the entire nation toward self-respect, self-rule, and pride.

The glorious lives of these tribal leaders and freedom fighters have been brought into vibrant being across sixteen galleries, utilizing modern digital arts. Our purpose is to keep the memory of these noble spirits perpetually alive, to render their epic struggles visible to every citizen, and to sow the seeds of their ideals as an unending fount of inspiration. Every document, every stroke of artwork, and all the narratives enshrined within these walls serve as a powerful testament to their unwavering devotion, fierce valor, and deep commitment to the public weal.

This museum serves as a guiding beacon for future generations, connecting them directly to their heritage, fostering a deep respect for nature, and inspiring them to remain vigilant in defending their identity. The tribal community's spiritual bond with jal, jungle, and zameen transcends a mere definition of their existence; it is, in fact, an enduring expression of human consciousness itself.

We envision this museum as more than a historical source - it is intended as a dynamic academy where future generations will gain an intimate understanding of their ancestors' sacrifices, culture, and values.
Hina Animesh Netam
